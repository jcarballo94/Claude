# The teaser campaign

Six little "movie trailer" GIFs to text Eli over the day, ending with the link. Send each GIF on its own first, then the line under it as a separate text.

| When | GIF | Text to send after it |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | `1-coming.gif` | 👀 |
| Late morning | `2-starring.gif` | casting has been finalized |
| Lunch | `3-introducing.gif` | the role was written for you. no auditions were held. |
| Afternoon | `4-rating.gif` | it's been approved. you're the only person on earth allowed to watch it. |
| Early evening | `5-reviews.gif` | early reviews are in 🍿 |
| The moment | `6-nowplaying.gif` | Turn your sound on and hold your phone upright ❤️ https://jcarballo94.github.io/Claude/ |

## Tips
- If she asks what it is, stay mysterious: "you'll see 😌"
- Want it in one go instead? Send all six a few minutes apart, like a trailer rolling out.
- On iPhone: save a GIF to Photos, then in Messages tap the ➕ (or the Photos button) and pick it. It sends animated.
