# The teaser campaign

Little "movie release" pieces to text Eli over the day, ending with the link. Send each GIF or video on its own, then the line next to it as a separate text.

## Full day

| When | Send | Then text |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | `1-coming.gif` | 👀 |
| Mid-morning | `loading-12.gif` | (nothing, let it sit) |
| Late morning | `2-starring.gif` | casting has been finalized |
| Lunch | `3-introducing.gif` | the role was written for you. no auditions were held. |
| Early afternoon | `loading-67.gif` | still loading… |
| Afternoon | `bonus-bloopers.gif` | production has been rough today |
| Mid-afternoon | `4-rating.gif` | it's been approved. you're the only person on earth allowed to watch it. |
| Late afternoon | `5-reviews.gif` | early reviews are in 🍿 |
| Early evening | `official-trailer.mp4` | the official trailer just dropped 🎬 sound on |
| A bit later | `loading-99.gif`, then a minute later `loading-99wait.gif` | … |
| The moment | `6-nowplaying.gif` | Turn your sound on and hold your phone upright ❤️ https://jcarballo94.github.io/Claude/ |

## Short version (one evening)

`1-coming.gif` → `bonus-bloopers.gif` → `official-trailer.mp4` → `loading-99wait.gif` → `6-nowplaying.gif` with the link. Space them 10–20 minutes apart.

## Tips
- If she asks what it is, stay mysterious: "you'll see 😌"
- On iPhone: save each file to Photos, then in Messages tap the Photos button and pick it. GIFs send animated, and the trailer sends as a video with sound.
- The trailer is 38 seconds, vertical, and only uses the first four chapters plus one starry shot. It doesn't show the kiss or the ending.

## Extras

- `poster.png`: a movie poster sized for an iPhone wallpaper (1180×2560). Send it after she's seen the story: "the official poster. set it as your wallpaper, it's required 😌"
- `../stickers/`: 12 animated stickers with transparent backgrounds for everyday texting: hi, miss you, mwah, sorry, hungry?, good morning, good night, proud of you, come home, love you, ur cute, haha.
