# Surprise for Eli ❤️

A tiny interactive pixel-art love story, made for one person.

Everything is in **`index.html`**: the art, animation, music and sound effects are all generated in code. There are no images, audio files or libraries to go missing.

## Preview it

- **Quickest:** open `index.html` in any browser.
- **On your iPhone:** host it (see below), open the link in Safari, then choose Share → **Add to Home Screen** so it opens full-screen like an app.

## Host it (free, about 2 minutes)

**GitHub Pages:** in this repo go to Settings → Pages → Source: *Deploy from a branch*, pick your branch and `/ (root)`, then save. After about a minute you get a link like `https://<you>.github.io/<repo>/`.

Or drag the file onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to get an instant link.

## Tips before you hand her the phone

- Turn the volume up. The music starts when she taps **Start our story** and works even with the silent switch on. The 🔊 button in the corner mutes it.
- Hold the phone upright (portrait).
- Every scene waits for a tap, so she sets the pace.

## Testing shortcuts (for you, not her)

Add `?at=` to the URL to jump straight to a part:
`?at=market`, `?at=walk`, `?at=hill`, `?at=finale`, `?at=surprise`
